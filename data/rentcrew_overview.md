# RentCrew Platform Knowledge Base

## Overview
RentCrew is a next-generation peer-to-peer and commercial rental platform connecting equipment owners, property hosts, and renters worldwide. The platform offers instant verified bookings, escrow payment protection, automated dispute resolution, and on-demand customer support.

## Booking & Reservation Policy
1. **Instant Booking**: Users with verified identity profiles (KYC verified) can reserve available inventory immediately.
2. **Deposit & Escrow**: When a reservation is placed, the total rental fee plus a refundable security deposit is held in secure escrow. The deposit is automatically refunded within 48 hours of item return inspection.
3. **Cancellation Terms**:
   - Cancellations made more than 48 hours prior to reservation start time receive a 100% full refund.
   - Cancellations made between 24 and 48 hours prior receive a 50% refund of the rental fee; security deposit is 100% refunded.
   - Cancellations within 24 hours of the rental start time are non-refundable for the daily rate; security deposit is 100% refunded.

## Equipment & Condition Reporting
- Renters and owners must complete a digital pre-rental checklist including timestamped photos before the rental handover.
- Any pre-existing damage must be documented in the RentCrew app within 2 hours of receiving the equipment.
